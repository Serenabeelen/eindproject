Extracting DNA from strawberries

What is DNA

DNA is also called deoxyribonucleic acid. It is found in the cells of all living organisms. DNA holds the instructions or blueprints for our bodies. Each cell has an entire copy of these instructions in it's nucleus.

Scientific explanation

The extractionliquid containing salt, detergent and smashed strawberries open up the strawberry cells. The DNA inside the cells release. Salt clumbs up the cells. Alcohol seperates the DNA from the straberry liquid.

Instructions

1.Chill the rubbing alcohol in the Freezer. Must be really cold!
2. Mix water, salt and dishwashingliquid in a bowl. This is your extractionliquid
3. Fill funnel with cheesecloth. Put funnel into an empty tall drinking glass.
4. Cut the green part of the strawberry off and put the remaing strawberry in a plasticbag. Mush it with your fingers into a strawberry juice like consistency.
5. add 3 tablespoons of the extractionliquid into the bag
6. Pour the straberry mixture into the funnel and let it drip
7. Pour the filtered straberry liquid into a small jar until a quarter is filled
8. slowly pour alcohol into the cup until you have a layer of 2.5cm alcohol liquid on top. DO NOT MIX!
9. Grab a skewer and pull up the white strings inside the rubbing alcohol layer.
