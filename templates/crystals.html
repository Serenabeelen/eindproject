What is are rocksugar crystals?

Rock candy, is literally sugarcrystals on a stick.
Rocksugar crystals or rock candy is formed by a super saturated sugar solution to crystallize onto a surface suitable for crystal nucleation.

Scientific explanation

The crystallization process consists of two steps: nucleation and crystalgrowth. During crystallization sugaratoms bind together with well defined angles to form a characteristic crystal shape with smooth surfaces and facets
Nucleationstep (the initial process in crystal formation from a solution, liquid, or vapor ):
Sugarmolecules dispersed in water start to gather into cluster, on microscopic scale.
Crystal growth: From the from the nucleus, sugarmolecules arrange into a highly ordered, solid crystalline structure, forming the rock sugar attached to the sticks.

Instructions

1. Bring water to a boil (100 ℃)
2. Add half of the sugar, when it's dissolved add the other half and let it dissolve
3. Allow sugarmixture to cool for 15 minutes
4. When the sugarmixture is cooling wet your sticks with water and cover them in sugar. This will allow the sugarmolecules to stick on the woodenstick.
5. Add a few drops of foodcoloring and flavoring (both optional) to each glass.
6. Add the sugarsolution to each glass and stir.
7. Stick the sugared sticks in place a peg on top of each and place into the glass. 8. Wait for 1 to 2 weeks. After 2-3 days your sugarcrystals will grow.
9. Gently break the top with a sharp object. Remove skewer and hang upside down until the excess liquid drips off and it dries.
9. Enjoy eating them or you can also use them as decoration!
