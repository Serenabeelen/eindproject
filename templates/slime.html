What is slime

Slime is used as a toy for kids, but can also be used to remove dust. It's a non-Newtonian liquid that you can hold and play around with.

Scientific explanation

A non-Newtonian liquid is a liquid which the viscosity changes when stress is applied. Viscocity means the thickness or stickyness of a liquid.
Baking soda is used in slime as activator. It is used to make the slime thick.
Contact lens solution In glue is a substance called polyvinylacetate that reacts with borate ions in the contact solution. When this happens the polyvinylacetate changes in strcture crosslinking together turning the glue into a stretchy slime like texture

Instructions

1. Put the glue inside a bowl with some foodcoloring and glitter.
2. Add the baking soda with the glue and mix until it's a smooth mixture.
3. Put the lens solution with the mixture, slowly until you get slimy threads. The mixture is now hardening.
4. Keep mixxing slowly until you get a ball of slime.
5. Grab the slime from the bowl and knead until smooth. If it's too sticky add half a tablespoon of contact solution.
6. Store in an airtight container.
