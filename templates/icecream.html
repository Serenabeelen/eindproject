Icecream

⏲ 30minutes

Ingrediënts

Equipment

Icecream in a bag

Do you like fresh icecream and do you not want to buy an expensive icecream maker? This recipe is for you! With a bit of science you can make delicious icecream at home with just a few ingrediënts and you can add whatever toppings you like!

Scientific explanation

Ice is about 0℃ which is the freezingpoint of water. Salt has a lower meltingpoint. When you mix salt and ice with each other the temperature is much lower than 0℃. The temperature will be closer to -20℃. This allows you to make icecream in about 15 minutes! It will also give you a great armworkout so you don't feel bad about eating the icecream afterwards 😉

Instructions

1. Mix cream, sugar, vanilla extract and toppings inside a large ziploc bag. Seal the bag pressing as much air out as you can
2. In a different bag add the ice and salt and mix.
3. Add the ziplog bag with icecream mixture inside the ice/salt bag. Seal the bag pressing as much air out as you can.
4. Shake the bag for 5-15 minutes until you have an icecream consistency.
5. Take the bag out of the ice/salt bag and transfer the contents of the icecream into a container to enjoy!
