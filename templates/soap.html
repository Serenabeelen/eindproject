What is soap and why is it so good at cleaning

Soap is a surfactant and it's made with mixing lye (sodiumhydroxide) with oil. Soap solubilizes particles and grimes in micells. Which are molecules with a hydrophic tail (afraid of water) and hydrophylic head(likes water). A micell traps the dirt inside the core while it can still be in solution.

Scientific explanation

Soap is made with a process called saponification. The oils used in the soap have triglycerides on their molecular structure. When lye reacts with it it's releasing fatty acid salts and glycerol.

Instructions

1. Put the sheabutter in a bowl in the microwave until it has melted.
2. Add the oliveoil and coconut oil the bowl and mix.
3. Make a sodiumhydroxide solution with water by slowly adding the sodiumhydroxide to the water. Be carefull this mixture is highly corrosive and it gets hot!
4. Slowly add the sodiumhydroxide mixture to the oil while you mix that with a handmixer, mix the perfumeoil when the sodiumhydroxide mixture is added.
5. Mix for 1 minute.
6. Put the soap mixture in mall.
