How to make a compass

⏲ 20minutes

Ingrediënts

Equipment

What is a compass

A compass is a tool that helps us follow direction to north, east, south and west. North is the direction that points to the northphole.

Scientific explanation

Earth is a giant magnet and the compass is a free smaller magnet, so the magnet needle on a compass always points towards the North pole. So basically the spinning magnet aligns itself it Earth's magnetic field.

Instructions

1. Cut your cork with a knife so it's about 2cm thick.
2. Rub needle against a magnet in the same direction for 20 times.
3. Stick needle all the way through your cork or foam
4. Fill the bowl with water and place cork with needle in the bowl
5. Place your compass next to the bowl and mark where north, east, west and south is. One needle end should point towards the Northpole and the other end points South.
