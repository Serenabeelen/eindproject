Slushie

⏲ 3 hours and 15 minutes

Ingrediënts

Equipment

Instant slushie without a slushiemachine

Do you have patience and do you like to have a cold beverage? This experiment will show you an easy way to make a slushie without expensive equipment. The only things required are a freezer and a bottle of your favorite fizzy soda.

Scientific explanation

When you cool the bottle of soda in the freezer the soda is chilled below its freezing point without turning into ice. The reason why is it is because soda is a mixture of carbondioxide, sugar and water. The carbon dioxide interferes with the ice forming. When you open the bottle after freezing the pressure drops and the bubbles release. Creating a surface for the ice to form inside the bottle.

Instructions

1. Shake your room temperature bottle of soda heavily.
2. Put the bottle in the freezer for 3 hours and 15 minutes.
3. Take the bottle out of the freezer, release the air, tighten the cap and see the soda turn into an instant slushie.
4. Enjoy your treat!
