What is a lavalamp

Edward Craven Walker a britisch entrepreneur invented a decorative lamp called a lava lamp in 1963. They consists of a clear transluscent liquid with wax in a glass vessel. The clear translucent liquid used was tetrachloride, but has not been used since 1970. Tetrachloride is banned due to toxicity. What they use now as liquid is a trade secret but some sources only say they add mineral oil, water and other chemicals to change the density of the liquid.
Fun fact: The company Cloudfare uses the unpredictable movements of lavalamps to use as the foundation of encryptionkeys. Protecting a massive portion of the internet!

Scientific explanation

A lavalamp has wax and a liquid (probably water, and preservatives). The wax when heated expands and becomes less dense. The melted wax rises, causing blobs to ascend to the top of the lamp. A metallic wire coil in the bottle's base breaks the cooled blobs surface tension, allowing them to merge and repeat the cycle.

Instructions

1. Add for 1/4th of water into your bottle with a few drops of foodcoloring.
2. Fill the remaining space with sunfloweroil.
3. Add 1/4th of a vitamin C effervescent tablet to the mixture and let the magic begin!
4. make the room dark and but a flashlight under the bottle for a more beautiful effect.
