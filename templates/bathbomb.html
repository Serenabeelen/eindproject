Bathbombs

This is a tutorial on how you can make your homemade bathbombs! Bathbombs can be really expensive when you buy them from LUSH and making them at home is a fun activity!
What you will need is: -Bath bomb molds or icecube molds -110 gram bakingsoda -60 gram corn starch -60 grams of Epsom salt -1 or 11/2 teaspoon water 5-10 drops essential oil of your choice (lavender essential oil is good for relaxation) -1 teaspoon of oil -1-2 drops food coloring (optional) {% endblock %}
