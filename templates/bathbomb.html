How to make bathbombs at home

Home / Experiments / Cosmetics / Bathbomb

⏲ 30 minutes

This recipe is for 2 bathbombs

Ingrediënts

Equipment

What is bathbomb

Bathbombs are balls that you can throw in your bath, made with sodiumbicarbonate, citric acid and other ingrediënts to make your bath experience a lot of fun. Bathbombs can be made in various colors and fragrances.

Benefits

Depending on the added ingredients, bathbombs can help soothe your skin or make you more relaxed. For kids it is a lot of fun to have a bathbomb color the bath in vibrant colors.

Scientific explanation

Sodiumbicarbonate reacts with the citric acid forming carbondioxide, water and sodiumcitrate.
Like this: sodiumbicarbonate + citric acid → carbondioxide + water + sodiumcitrate
The fizzing in the water is the carbon dioxide forming when the reaction happens.
The cornstarch(maizena) acts as a filler, it affects the reactionrate of the bathbombs reaction. The faster the bathbomb dissolves the more carbondioxide it will make thus making the bathbomb fizzier.
The epsomsalt is used to relax your muscles and soothes your skin

Instructions

1. In a big bowl combine the bakingsoda, maizena, citric acid and epsom salt and mix.
2. In a small jar stir the water, oil, foodcoloring and essential oil.
3. slowly pour the mixing liquid in the big bowl of dry ingrediënts with a whisk. If you can make a solid clumb in your hands it's ready.
4. If it's too powdery add a tiny amount of water until it holds together.
5. Fill your bath bomb molds with the mixture until it is slightly overflowing and press both sides together. Remove the mold and place the bath bomb to dry on a tray.
6. Let it dry overnight or for 8 hours. Store them in a dry place.
