What is a facemask

It's a type of sheetmask, but made with a gel-like texture that feels soothing for the skin.

Scientific explanation

Agar agar is a seaweed derived substance from red algae or Irish mosh. It consists of polysaccharide agarose (sugar) and agaropectin. It is found in the cell walls of the seaweeds. When you heat up agar agar, a gel will form because the molecules will have a helix-formation. When it ages, the helixes will form stronger bonds.
The cucumberjuice, green tea and glycerin are used for the soothing properties. You can always experiment further and add some other ingredients like honey or aloe vera.

Instructions

1. Heat up the water until it is 90 Celsius.
2. Add the agar agar powder and stir until the solution is clear.
3. Let the agar agar cool down to 50 celcius and add the cucumberjuice and glycerin, stir.
4. Transfer the solution into the facemask molds and refridgerate for 10 minutes.
5. Take out the facemask and enjoy!
