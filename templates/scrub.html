Why use a bodyscrub

Scrubbing your body helps exfoliate the skin. This deepcleases the skin bariër, making your skin soft and promote cell turnover.

Different types of exfoliation

Exfoliation can be done in many ways, like using mechanic exfoliation with abrasive material. This can be a sponges, brushes, salt, sugar etc.
Chemical exfoliation works different for example acids and enzymes that bond to the dead skin cells and removing them. They exfoliate deeper into the skin(pores) and dissolve all the gunk and oil as well.
