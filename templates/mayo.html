What is mayonaise

Mayo is used as condiment for sandwiches, fries and other edible goods.

Scientific explanation

Mayonaise is made by mixing two liquids that do not mix by using emulsion. Emulsion can happen by rapid mixing. Eggs are the emulsifiër which contains lecithin. When you add the egg to oil and water the emulsification begins. A thick substance forms and mayo is made.

Instructions

1. Put all the ingrediënts except the oil in a jar and mix with the handmixer.
2. Slowly add a bit of oil and mix, repeat this until you see the oil binding.
3. If you want it thicker you can always add more oil. Enjoy!
